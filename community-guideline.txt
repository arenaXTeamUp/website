# Community Guidelines

Last Updated: May 18, 2026

Welcome to ArenaX.

ArenaX is a platform designed to help players and organizers discover, create, and manage local tournaments and gaming communities in a safe, respectful, and enjoyable environment.

By using ArenaX, you agree to follow these Community Guidelines.

--------------------------------------------------

# 1. Respect Other Users

Users must treat other players, organizers, teams, and community members respectfully.

The following behavior is not allowed:

- Harassment or bullying
- Hate speech or discrimination
- Threats or intimidation
- Personal attacks or abusive language
- Toxic or disruptive behavior

--------------------------------------------------

# 2. Honest & Fair Use

Users must use ArenaX honestly and responsibly.

The following activities are prohibited:

- Fake tournaments
- Fraudulent activity
- Impersonation of individuals or organizations
- False tournament information
- Misleading participants
- Artificial manipulation of rankings or results
- Exploiting bugs or platform vulnerabilities

--------------------------------------------------

# 3. Tournament Rules

Tournament organizers are responsible for ensuring:

- Accurate tournament details
- Fair participation rules
- Clear scheduling and communication
- Proper prize handling
- Respectful event management

ArenaX may remove tournaments that are misleading, harmful, abusive, or violate platform policies.

--------------------------------------------------

# 4. Prohibited Content

Users must not upload, publish, or share content that includes:

- Illegal material
- Hate speech
- Violent or graphic content
- Sexually explicit content
- Copyright-infringing material
- Spam or scam content
- Malware, harmful links, or malicious files
- Content promoting illegal activities

--------------------------------------------------

# 5. Real-World Safety

ArenaX helps users participate in local physical tournaments and events.

Users should:

- Use caution when attending offline events
- Verify tournament details independently
- Avoid sharing sensitive personal information publicly
- Report suspicious or unsafe behavior

ArenaX is not responsible for offline interactions, disputes, or incidents between users or organizers.

--------------------------------------------------

# 6. No Gambling or Betting

ArenaX does not allow:

- Gambling
- Betting
- Wagering
- Unauthorized real-money gaming activities within the app

ArenaX only provides tools for tournament discovery, organization, and participation.

--------------------------------------------------

# 7. Account & Platform Abuse

Users must not:

- Attempt unauthorized access to accounts or systems
- Reverse engineer or exploit the platform
- Use bots or automated abuse tools
- Disrupt platform operations
- Circumvent restrictions or moderation actions

Any malicious or abusive activity may result in account suspension or permanent bans.

--------------------------------------------------

# 8. User Content Responsibility

Users are solely responsible for the content they create, upload, or publish on ArenaX.

This includes:

- Tournament details
- Team names
- Images
- Community posts
- Profile information

Users must ensure they have the rights to any content they upload.

--------------------------------------------------

# 9. Reporting Violations

Users can report:

- Fake tournaments
- Harassment
- Abuse
- Fraudulent activity
- Unsafe behavior
- Policy violations

ArenaX may review reported content and take action where necessary.

--------------------------------------------------

# 10. Enforcement Actions

ArenaX reserves the right to:

- Remove content
- Restrict tournament visibility
- Suspend accounts
- Permanently ban users
- Limit access to platform features

Actions may be taken without prior notice when necessary for platform safety, legal compliance, or community protection.

--------------------------------------------------

# 11. Changes to Guidelines

These Community Guidelines may be updated from time to time.

Continued use of ArenaX after updates means you accept the revised guidelines.

--------------------------------------------------
