Welcome to ArenaX.

ArenaX is a platform for discovering, creating, and managing local, skill-based sports tournaments. Our goal is to foster a safe, respectful, and fair community for all players and organizers. By using ArenaX, you agree to follow these Community Guidelines.

--------------------------------------------------

1. Respect Other Users
Treat all players, organizers, teams, and community members with respect. The following is strictly prohibited:
- Harassment or bullying
- Hate speech or discrimination
- Threats or intimidation
- Personal attacks or abusive language
- Toxic or disruptive behavior

2. Honest & Fair Use
Use ArenaX honestly and responsibly. Prohibited activities include:
- Creating fake tournaments or misleading listings
- Fraudulent activity or impersonation
- False tournament information (incorrect venue, prize, or schedule)
- Artificially manipulating rankings or results
- Exploiting bugs or platform vulnerabilities

3. Tournament Rules & Organizer Responsibility
Tournament organizers must ensure:
- All details are accurate and truthful
- Rules are fair and clearly communicated
- The event is a genuine, on-field skill-based sports competition (e.g., cricket, football, badminton, volleyball)
- Prizes (if any) are awarded as promised, directly by the organizer
- The tournament does not involve any form of gambling, betting, or wagering

By creating a tournament, organizers confirm it complies with these guidelines. ArenaX may remove tournaments that are misleading, harmful, or violate platform policies.

4. Prohibited Content
Do not upload, post, or share content that contains:
- Illegal material
- Hate speech or discriminatory content
- Violent or graphic imagery
- Sexually explicit material
- Copyright-infringing files
- Spam, scams, or deceptive content
- Malware, harmful links, or malicious files
- Content promoting illegal activities, including gambling or betting

5. Real-World Safety
ArenaX helps you find local physical events. When attending offline tournaments:
- Verify tournament details independently
- Exercise caution and avoid sharing sensitive personal information publicly
- Report any suspicious or unsafe behavior
ArenaX is not responsible for offline interactions, disputes, or incidents between users.

6. No Gambling or Betting
ArenaX is a platform exclusively for skill-based sports tournaments. We strictly prohibit:
- Gambling
- Betting
- Wagering
- Speculative games or any activity where the outcome relies predominantly on chance

All tournaments must involve actual on-field play where skill determines the winner. Any listing that promotes gambling or betting will be removed immediately, and the responsible account may be permanently banned.

7. Account & Platform Abuse
You must not:
- Attempt unauthorized access to accounts or systems
- Reverse engineer or exploit the App
- Use bots or automated tools for abuse
- Disrupt platform operations
- Circumvent moderation actions or restrictions

8. User Content Responsibility
You are solely responsible for the content you create or share, including:
- Tournament descriptions
- Team names and logos
- Images and media
- Profile information
Ensure you have the rights to all content you upload and that it complies with these guidelines.

9. Reporting Violations
Help us keep ArenaX safe. You can report:
- Fake or misleading tournaments
- Harassment or abuse
- Gambling or betting-related content
- Fraud or scams
- Unsafe behavior
- Any policy violation

Reports can be submitted through in-app options or by contacting support.

10. Enforcement Actions
ArenaX reserves the right to:
- Remove violating content or tournaments
- Restrict tournament visibility
- Suspend or permanently ban accounts
- Limit access to features

Actions may be taken without prior notice when necessary to protect the community, comply with law, or ensure platform safety.

11. Changes to Guidelines
These Community Guidelines may be updated from time to time. Your continued use of ArenaX after changes means you accept the updated guidelines.
