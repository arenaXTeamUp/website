
# Privacy Policy


Last Updated: May 18, 2026

Welcome to ArenaX.

This Privacy Policy explains how ArenaX (“we,” “our,” or “us”) collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the ArenaX mobile application and related services.

By downloading, accessing, or using ArenaX, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

--------------------------------------------------

# 1. Information We Collect

## a) Account Information

When users create or access an account, ArenaX may collect:

- Name or display name
- Email address
- Profile photo
- Phone number (if added or verified by the user)
- Team information
- Region and profile preferences
- Social profile links (optional)

## b) Google Sign-In Information

When users sign in using Google authentication, ArenaX may collect:

- Google account name
- Email address
- Profile photo

Email addresses remain private and are not publicly visible to other users.

Public profile information such as display names and profile photos may be visible within tournaments, teams, or community sections.

## c) Tournament & Community Information

ArenaX may collect and process:

- Tournament participation history
- Team details
- Match results
- Rankings and leaderboard information
- Tournament details created by users
- Community content and reports
- Organizer information related to tournaments

Certain tournament and organizer information may be publicly visible to other users inside the app.

## d) Device & Technical Information

We may automatically collect technical information including:

- Device model
- Operating system version
- App version
- IP address
- Device identifiers
- Crash logs
- Diagnostics data
- Basic usage analytics

## e) Location Information

ArenaX requires location access to help users:

- Discover nearby tournaments
- Apply radius-based tournament filters
- Receive local tournament recommendations
- View tournament venues and nearby events

Without location access, certain core features of the app may not function properly.

ArenaX does not access location data in the background when the app is closed.

## f) Camera Permission

ArenaX may request camera access to allow users to:

- Scan tournament QR codes
- Join tournaments during physical events
- Verify tournament participation

Camera access is only used when users actively use tournament scanning features.

ArenaX uses collected information to:

- Create and manage user accounts

- Support QR code scanning and event participation
- Improve app performance and user experience
- Detect fraud, abuse, cheating, or unlawful activity
- Communicate important updates, notifications, and support messages
- Maintain platform security and integrity
